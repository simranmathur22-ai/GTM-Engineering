Create evidence-grounded outreach for a CRM PII Exposure Scanner. Four specialist agents work in sequence; you stay in control of every draft.

Demo product: a scanner concept that helps CRM teams find potential personal information in free-text fields for human review. This app does not scan CRM data, identify actual incidents, or provide legal advice. Email drafts are copied to HubSpot for you to review and send there; this app never sends them.

Uses public company research and the local 7-day cache. A first run can take a few minutes on rate-limited API plans.
